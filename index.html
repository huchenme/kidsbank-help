KidsBank Support

Contact support

Email chen.hu.sg@gmail.com with your app version, device model, and what happened. If you send a screenshot, remove children’s names, photos, and other private details. Keep your passwords and parent PIN private.

Family records are missing

Confirm the device is signed in to iCloud and connected to the internet. Open KidsBank on both devices and give a recent change time to sync. If records are still missing, contact support before reinstalling.

Notifications are missing

Allow notifications for KidsBank in iOS Settings and check the notification preferences inside KidsBank. If a device has an older copy of a task, a notification may show a general message.

Coins and rewards

Coins are records that families manage. Parents handle offline purchases and provide the rewards agreed with their children.

Privacy and removal

Email chen.hu.sg@gmail.com for help with a privacy or data-removal request. Keep private family records out of the first message.

小小金库帮助中心

联系支持

请发邮件至 chen.hu.sg@gmail.com，并附上应用版本、设备型号及问题经过。发送截图前，请遮住孩子的姓名、照片和其他私人信息。请妥善保管密码和家长 PIN 码。

家庭记录没有同步

确认设备已登录 iCloud，且网络连接正常。在两台设备上打开小小金库，稍等片刻，看看最近修改的内容是否已同步。若仍找不到记录，请先联系支持。

收不到通知

请到 iOS 的「设置」中允许小小金库发送通知，并查看应用内的通知偏好。如果接收设备上的任务资料还没更新，通知可能只显示通用提示。

金币与奖励

金币由家庭自行记录。线下购买及与孩子约定的奖励由家长处理。

隐私与删除资料

如需咨询隐私或申请删除资料，请发邮件至 chen.hu.sg@gmail.com。首次联系时请先省略家庭私密记录。
