KidsBank Privacy Policy

Effective 22 November 2026. Applies to the KidsBank 1.0.0 App Store release.

Who operates KidsBank

KidsBank is operated by Hu Chen. You can contact me at chen.hu.sg@gmail.com.

Family information

KidsBank uses family and child profiles, photos or icons you choose, tasks, goals, check-in notes, rewards, and coin records to provide the family features. If you invite a child account, the app uses the email address you enter to set up the invitation.

iCloud and family sharing

KidsBank syncs family records using Apple iCloud and CloudKit. When you share a family, access depends on the recipient’s family and device roles. Records already stored in iCloud or shared with others can remain after you remove the app.

Notifications

If you use family notifications, Apple Push Notification service and a Cloudflare relay process delivery. The relay processes device notification tokens, internal identifiers, permissions, preferences and delivery records. Detailed previews can use task information already on the receiving device.

Product analytics

The App Store release has optional product analytics turned off. Earlier TestFlight builds may have sent product usage events, app language, and pseudonymous record identifiers to PostHog for product analytics. Contact support for help with data from a TestFlight build.

Support and removal

We use the email and details you send to help with your request. Use the app’s controls to manage or delete family records. Shared copies may remain with other members. To stop notifications, change your preferences in KidsBank or iOS Settings. You can also remove a connected device in KidsBank. The relay uses registration and access records to deliver notifications and enforce permissions. Older delivery logs are periodically pruned. The support inbox keeps the information needed to handle an active request and any follow-up. Contact chen.hu.sg@gmail.com for help with access, privacy or removal.

Family use

Parents and guardians manage the family and can review what children enter or share through their family devices.

小小金库隐私政策

生效日期为 2026 年 11 月 22 日。适用于 App Store 上发布的小小金库 1.0.0 版。

运营者

小小金库由 Hu Chen 运营。您可以发邮件至 chen.hu.sg@gmail.com 联系我。

家庭资料

小小金库使用家庭和孩子的档案、所选照片或图标、任务、目标、打卡备注、奖励和金币记录，为家庭提供相关功能。邀请孩子的账户时，应用会使用您输入的电子邮箱地址来处理邀请。

iCloud 与家庭共享

小小金库通过 Apple 的 iCloud 和 CloudKit 同步家庭记录。共享家庭后，成员可访问的资料取决于其家庭和设备角色。卸载应用后，已存入 iCloud 或与他人共享的资料可能仍被保留。

通知

如使用家庭通知，Apple 推送通知服务与 Cloudflare 中继会处理通知的发送。中继会处理设备通知令牌、内部标识符、权限、通知偏好和发送记录。详细通知预览可使用已经保存在接收设备上的任务资料。

产品使用分析

App Store 版本关闭了可选的产品使用分析。较早的 TestFlight 版本可能向 PostHog 发送产品使用事件、应用语言以及经过假名化处理的记录标识符，用于分析产品使用情况。如需处理测试版产生的资料，请联系支持。

支持与删除资料

我们使用您提供的邮箱及相关信息来处理您的请求。您可以通过应用内的功能管理或删除家庭记录，其他成员仍可能保有共享副本。如需停止接收通知，可在小小金库或 iOS「设置」中调整通知偏好，也可以在小小金库中移除已连接的设备。通知中继使用设备登记和访问权限记录来发送通知、控制访问，并会定期清理较早的发送记录。支持邮箱会保留处理请求及后续沟通所需的信息。如需访问资料、咨询隐私或申请删除，请联系 chen.hu.sg@gmail.com。

家庭使用

家长或监护人管理家庭，也可以查看孩子在家庭设备上输入或共享的内容。
